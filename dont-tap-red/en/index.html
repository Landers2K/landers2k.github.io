Game modes

Four ways to play.
One single rule.

01 Arcade

Chain combos. Beat your best.

Tap the blue cubes, make your combo explode and unlock crazier and crazier bonuses: FREEZE, BOMB, x2, extra life and golden star cubes… Surprises change the rules mid-game. Can you reach LEGEND rank?

02 Rain

Cubes fall from the sky.

Catch the right cubes before they hit the ground, dodge the red ones and survive the events: lightning, night, storm… Surprise challenges during the game earn you even more points.

03 Fusion

Merge up to the gold cube.

Drop the cubes into the barrel: two identical cubes merge into a bigger one. Climb all the way to the gold cube without crossing the red line, with 3 bonuses to get you out of tight spots: Swap, Shake and the Smash hammer.

04 Chrono

60 seconds, not one more.

No lives to manage: just a timer and cubes. Chain as many right cubes as you can to blow up your score before time runs out.

FAQ

Everything you want to know.

Is the game free?

Yes. Don’t Tap Red is free on iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and Android. It contains ads, which you can remove forever with a one-time purchase, plus optional purchases such as packs and skins.

When did the game come out?

It came out on October 21, 2026 on iPhone and on October 22, 2026 on Android, then on November 11, 2026 on iPad and iPhone Duo with the 3.0.2 update: it’s free on the App Store and on Google Play.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone, iPhone Duo (open or folded), iPad, and Android phones and tablets. The game is available in English and French.

How do I use a gift code?

In the game, go to SETTINGS, tap GIFT CODE and enter your code. The reward lands in your account right away.

How do the leaderboards work?

In Arcade, Rain, Chrono and Fusion, your best score counts in the world, weekly and monthly leaderboards. The top 10 of each mode gets rewards every week, straight to your mailbox.

I found a bug or I have an idea: what do I do?

Email donttapred.contact@gmail.com or come and talk about it on Discord.

What about my personal data?

Everything is explained in the game’s privacy policy (in French).
