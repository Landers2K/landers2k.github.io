About

I’m Tony, but you probably know me as Landers2K. I’m 32 and I’m a content creator, streamer and indie video game developer.

  • 10+ yearsof passion for gaming and streaming
  • Sept. 2025the big leap: the gear and resources to take things up a notch
  • 3 worldsvideos, live streams and my own video games
  • 1st gameDon’t Tap Red, free on iPhone and Android

I’ve been passionate about gaming and streaming for more than 10 years, and in September 2025 I decided to take that passion to a new level, when I could finally invest in the gear and resources I needed to make my ambitions real.

Today, I share my universe through my videos on YouTube and TikTok, my live streams on Twitch and the development of my own video games, including Don’t Tap Red, free on iOS and Android.

Don’t Tap Red.

A fast-paced reflex game where every second counts. Cubes move, speed up and multiply: keep your cool, chain the right cubes and above all… never touch the red one!

All about the game

The goal

One single rule.

  • Tap the blue to score points and grow your combo.
  • Never touch the red: one wrong move and it’s over.

Game modes

Four ways to play.
One single rule.

01 Arcade

Chain combos. Beat your best.

Tap the blue cubes, make your combo explode and unlock crazier and crazier bonuses: FREEZE, BOMB, x2, extra life and golden star cubes… Surprises change the rules mid-game. Can you reach LEGEND rank?

02 Rain

Cubes fall from the sky.

Catch the right cubes before they hit the ground, dodge the red ones and survive the events: lightning, night, storm… Surprise challenges during the game earn you even more points.

03 Challenge

20 worlds to conquer.

Every world has its own rules and traps. Earn 3 stars everywhere, unlock Hard mode and take on the Daily Challenge, different every day.

04 Chrono

60 seconds, not one more.

No lives to manage: just a timer and cubes. Chain as many right cubes as you can to blow up your score before time runs out.

Missions

Titles

Events

Everyone against the Black Cube.

A giant boss that all players attack together: its HP drops live, and everyone is rewarded based on their place in the damage ranking. Not to mention the seasons that transform the game.

Leaderboards

Rewards every week and every month.

In Arcade, Rain and Chrono, your best score counts in the world, weekly and monthly leaderboards. Rewards land in your mailbox.
